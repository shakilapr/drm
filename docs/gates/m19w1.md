## Notes

- No architecture divergence: the implementation follows the pre-existing
  [spatial-core.md](../algorithms/spatial/spatial-core.md) pseudocode and
  [spatial-types-design.md](../spec/spatial-types-design.md); no ADR.
- `drm-workspace` may not depend on the `drm-spatial` runtime crate (codebase.md
  2.1), so the build-time fold carries a minimal rigid-transform type for
  codegen; the emitted constants are raw `[f64; 3]`/`[f64; 4]` and the runtime
  consumes them unchanged.
- The feature-gated recognizer from M15W1 is now compiled unconditionally (the
  Phase 33 grammar gate is active); the ordinary `.drm` corpus is unchanged.
- Regression permanence (AGENTS #14): every M19W1 suite becomes a blocking gate.
- Carry-over: clock-domain degradation paths ⇒ M19W2 (E2); cache consumption by
  queries ⇒ M19W2 (E1).
