## Notes

- No architecture divergence: the implementation follows the pre-existing
  [spatial-queries.md](../algorithms/spatial/spatial-queries.md) pseudocode and
  the [spatial-core.md](../algorithms/spatial/spatial-core.md) handle/authority
  sketches. The `PlanStep::Invert` step is an additive executor op needed to fold
  a reverse-traversed calibration edge as `(Nominal × Correction)⁻¹`; it is
  recorded in the [spatial-queries.md](../algorithms/spatial/spatial-queries.md)
  §Data Structures sketch. No ADR (the step algebra is not arch-affecting).
- Single-writer authority uses a std `File` advisory lock (`File::try_lock`), so
  `drm-spatial` stays `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` and brokerless — no daemon
  arbitrates authority. The lock is released by the kernel when the holder dies,
  reusing the [publisher-lifecycle](../algorithms/runtime/publisher-lifecycle.md)
  authority pattern. Pre-deployment, `V-SPAT` rejects a declaration with more
  than one writer on a dynamic edge.
- Jump-threshold policy: an update whose correction jump (translation delta norm
  + rotation delta radians) exceeds the declared threshold marks the slot
  discontinuous at its effective instant; a later sub-threshold update clears the
  marker. This documents the calibration-jump risk in the work plan.
- Carry-over / risk: multi-edge compiled-plan composition order (fold order vs
  textbook `SE(3)` collapse) remains open from [m20w2.md](m20w2.md) ⇒ M20W4
  review (E1/E2) for a possible ADR.
- Regression permanence (AGENTS #14): the M20W1/M20W2 suites and the M20W3
  `calibration` suite become blocking gates.
