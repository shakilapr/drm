## SPIKE — Phase 33 falsification review (E4, Day 5)

Likely falsifiers in the final evidence, ranked by likelihood × blast radius
(feeds the W8 qualification spike plan). None blocks the Phase 33 gate: each is
currently evidenced at the tested granularity, and each probe targets the
*scale/extremes* the gate suites do not yet drive.

| Rank | Falsifier | Likelihood × blast | Why it could falsify | W8 probe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Grid completeness at density extremes** | high × high | `spatial_proximity_complete` proves 0 misses over 10k random scenes, but the candidate-set factor and backward-shift probe path are exercised near the declared occupancy; at `max_objects` with adversarial fat-envelope overlap a superset cell could be skipped | saturate the cell table to `α_max` with colliding fat envelopes and compare against brute force at every scene density |
| 2 | **BVH live subtree swaps under a calibration storm** | med × high | `live_calibration_swap` proves a single bank swap; sustained recalibration racing queries could publish a partially rebuilt bank or exhaust both banks | continuous swap loop under concurrent queries; assert every query sees a coherent pre/post bank or `Busy` |
| 3 | **Cache version races** | med × high | `spatial_cache_shortcircuit`/`queries_cache_aging` prove version equality reuses geometry and staleness is re-derived, but an async-rate burst could admit a version vector that matches while the common coherent `t*` advances | randomized async-rate race harness vs the single-instant oracle over long wall-clock runs |
| 4 | **MCU constant drift** | low × med | `spatial_mcu_constant` compares the emitted C subset against the `SE(3)` reference within the `SPATIAL_REFERENCE` band; a long static chain could accumulate FMA-vs-separate error past the band | deep static-chain (≥ 16 segment) host↔MCU parity with the combined absolute+relative band |
| 5 | **Multi-edge rotated composition order** (carry-over M20 falsifier #1) | med × high | the compiled-plan fold composes path segments in path order; accepted multi-edge fixtures are translation-only, so a rotation across two edges could expose an order/inversion divergence | rotated 2-edge oracle fixtures + convention decision (ADR if divergent) |
| 6 | **Generational handle reuse (ABA)** | low × high | `spatial_calibration_generational_stale_refused` proves delete/recycle invalidation, but `u32` generation wraparound could alias a recycled slot | generation wrap-boundary fixtures; exhaustion path |
| 7 | **Proximity candidate-factor perf regression** | med × med | the factor is documented but not gated as a perf budget; a denser scene could raise p99 latency while staying complete | candidate-factor bench across density sweeps with a p99 budget |
